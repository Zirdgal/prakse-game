Rules/How to Play:

This is a rhythm game you just tap the notes when they are over your input arrows.

By default. The Keybinds are: A,W,S,D and Space. AWSD are for the note inputs. Space is for the super meter.

The Super meter grants a 5x score multiplier for a short period.

Gaining a combo increases the combo multiplier dependant on how well timed your input was.

Missing or inputting the wrong note will grant negative score
